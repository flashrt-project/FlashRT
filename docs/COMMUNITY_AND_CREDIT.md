# Our community / 我们的社区理念

FlashRT shares code, designs, and engineering experience to advance technology
together with the community. We welcome learning, reuse, improvements, and new
explorations, and value the creativity and effort behind every contribution.

We believe that open sharing, respect for original contributions, and sincere
collaboration help the open-source community move forward. We invite everyone
to uphold these principles together, so that knowledge flows freely,
contributions are respected, and new work continues to grow.

## 中文

FlashRT 希望通过开放代码、设计与工程经验，与社区共同推动技术进步。我们欢迎学习、
复用、改进与新的探索，也珍视每一份贡献背后的创造与付出。

我们相信，开放共享、尊重原创与真诚协作，让开源社区持续向前。我们愿与所有参与者
共同践行这些原则，让知识自由流动，让贡献得到尊重，让新的成果不断生长。
